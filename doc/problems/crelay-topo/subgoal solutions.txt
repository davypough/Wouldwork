Subgoal 3 -- Assemble the live beam station and close cycle 3
------------------------------------------------

Subgoal:

  (and (has-location agent1 location1)
       (has-location box1 location9)
       (on connector1 box1)
       (paired connector1 transmitter1)
       (paired connector1 receiver1)
       (on tray1 plate2)
       (ghost-stops-recorder))

Status: validated, 8 actions (35 cumulative)

Purpose:

  Assemble the live beam station during the third recorder cycle, return agent1
  to recorder1, and close the cycle normally.  Agent1* remains empty-handed at
  recorder1 throughout: its copied connector on plate1 is sufficient to hold
  gate1 open while agent1 relocates the live connector.  The live box, connector
  support, and pairings survive the closure, leaving a clean checkpoint from
  which a later START-RECORDER can fork the complete arrangement into the
  recording layer.

Integrated WouldWork action sequence from the Subgoal 2 endpoint:

   1. (MOVE AGENT1 ((WALK LOCATION9 (GATE1 GATE3) LOCATION1)))
   2. (START-RECORDER AGENT1)
   3. (MOVE AGENT1 ((WALK LOCATION1 NIL LOCATION2)))

   5. (MOVE AGENT1 ((WALK LOCATION2 (GATE1 GATE3) LOCATION9)))

         AGENT1 CONNECTOR1 LOCATION9 BOX1 (RECEIVER1 TRANSMITTER1))
