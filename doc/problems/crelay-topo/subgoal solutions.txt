Subgoal 1 -- Hold gate1 open with connector1
------------------------------------------------

Subgoal:

  (and (on connector1 plate1)
       (ghost-stops-recorder))

Status: validated, 10 actions

Purpose:

  Complete one normal recorder cycle with connector1 left on plate1 so that
  gate1 remains open.  Agent1 ends at location2.  Tray1 remains on plate2 at
  location7, keeping gate3 open, and box1 remains at location6.

Integrated WouldWork action sequence:

   1. (START-RECORDER AGENT1)
   2. (MOVE AGENT1* ((WALK LOCATION1 NIL LOCATION2)))
   3. (MOVE AGENT1* ((STEP (LOCATION2 GROUND) NIL
                                  (LOCATION2 PLATE1))))
   4. (MOVE AGENT1 ((WALK LOCATION1 (GATE1 GATE3) LOCATION9)))
   5. (PICKUP-CONNECTOR AGENT1 CONNECTOR1 LOCATION9)
   6. (MOVE AGENT1 ((WALK LOCATION9 (GATE1 GATE3) LOCATION2)))
   7. (PUT-CONNECTOR AGENT1 CONNECTOR1 LOCATION2 PLATE1)
   8. (MOVE AGENT1* ((STEP (LOCATION2 PLATE1) NIL
                                  (LOCATION2 GROUND))))
   9. (MOVE AGENT1* ((WALK LOCATION2 NIL LOCATION1)))
  10. (STOP-RECORDER AGENT1*)

Setup phase:

  No preliminary actions are required.  Initially agent1 is at recorder1 at
  location1, connector1 is at location9, tray1 is on plate2 at location7, and
  box1 is at location6.  Gates2, gate3, and gate5 are open.

Recording phase:

  (START-RECORDER AGENT1)
  (MOVE AGENT1* ((WALK LOCATION1 NIL LOCATION2)))
  (MOVE AGENT1* ((STEP (LOCATION2 GROUND) NIL
                              (LOCATION2 PLATE1))))
  (PAUSE)
  (MOVE AGENT1* ((STEP (LOCATION2 PLATE1) NIL
                              (LOCATION2 GROUND))))
  (MOVE AGENT1* ((WALK LOCATION2 NIL LOCATION1)))
  (STOP-RECORDER AGENT1*)

Playback phase during the pause:

  (MOVE AGENT1 ((WALK LOCATION1 (GATE1 GATE3) LOCATION9)))
  (PICKUP-CONNECTOR AGENT1 CONNECTOR1 LOCATION9)
  (MOVE AGENT1 ((WALK LOCATION9 (GATE1 GATE3) LOCATION2)))
  (PUT-CONNECTOR AGENT1 CONNECTOR1 LOCATION2 PLATE1)
  (RESUME)
